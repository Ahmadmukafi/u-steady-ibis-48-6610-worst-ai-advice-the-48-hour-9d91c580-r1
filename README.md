One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, delivered in its most confident and helpful voice:

> "If your phone falls in water, charge it immediately — the electricity dries it from the inside, much faster than rice."

The entry program (`main.ts`) writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke lands because the advice sounds tidy, specific, and plausibly helpful — right up until you follow it and fry the phone.
